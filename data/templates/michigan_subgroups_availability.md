# Michigan subgroup availability

Age subgroups (18–34, 35–54, and 55+) are loaded from the University of
Michigan's public demographic Table 1A. Each record pairs the Index of Consumer
Sentiment (ICS) with its published subgroup-specific `Cases` count. ICS values
are three-month moving averages. The displayed `Cases` value is retained as
source metadata; it is not asserted to be the pooled analytic base for the
three-month estimate.

No verified ICC/ICE component-subgroup series is loaded. A supplied archive
extract includes raw component responses through February 2026, but its
production sample-inclusion rule has not been confirmed against Table 1B.
The query for March–August 2026 returned no records on 2026-10-14. The
dashboard therefore freezes verified component-subgroup coverage at 2026-02
and reports March–August as pending producer or archive release. It also does
not yet load income, region, education, or political-party rows.
