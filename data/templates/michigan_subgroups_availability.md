No subgroup rows are loaded. The public August 2026 data booklet provides age,
income, region, and political-party index estimates, but does not provide a
documented sample size for each corresponding subgroup estimate. This project
requires both the estimate and sample size. Add records only from a licensed or
authorized University of Michigan export that supplies both fields, retaining
