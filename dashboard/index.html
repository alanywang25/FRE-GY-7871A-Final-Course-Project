Causal-design supplement

Observational association, not causal impact

The retrospective model uses prior-month validated negative tone, observed macro controls, outcome persistence, seasonality, HAC intervals, and a future-media placebo. It has no respondent-level exposure or exogenous treatment variation.

Data quality and interpretation

Important Caveats

  • Michigan Current Conditions and Expectations are final-value historical outcomes. Public age-group ICS is a three-month moving average. The dashboard does not claim verified subgroup ICC/ICE coverage after February 2026; the March–August archive query returned no records.
  • The Michigan survey changed from telephone RDD to address-based web collection during 2024; do not treat changes across that transition as directly comparable without a documented sensitivity analysis.
  • GDP remains quarterly; the pipeline does not interpolate it into artificial monthly observations.
